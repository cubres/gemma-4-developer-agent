## 2. Levers by topic

### 2.1 Thinking mode

- Evidence: the thinking-on row scored 0.12 (7 of 58). The thinking-off repeat had scored 0.06 (4 of 58). ### 2.2 Thinking budget and output cap

- Kept: 4096 and 8192, the family settings [V].
- Refuted as a second official arm: thinking 2048 with output 6144 (D v4h). The headroom it was meant to buy did not show in V20: zero context errors, largest prompt 14,993 tokens, and largest prompt plus 8,192 of 23,185, below the sweep's switch rule of 30,000 [V]. On dev data the 2048 arm scored 50 of 129 against 48 at 4096, with a gap of 0.9 of 58 and a standard error of 1.8 to 3.2 tasks [V, arithmetic]. The dev comparison ran at a 60-minute cap, where time did not bind [V]. D v4h was shelved on 10-10 [V].
- Held: D v4y (thinking 2048, output 8192). Central gain 0, range 0 to +0.5 [I]. It waits for an enforcement probe and a passing canary.
- Held: D v4x (output 6144 with thinking 4096). Gain 0 unless the overflow trigger fires, meaning a prompt above 24,576 tokens or a context error [V].
- Time model: a 2048 cap saves 0 seconds per 28-call task at 20 tokens per second, and about 34 seconds at 30 tokens per second [V, arithmetic].

### 2.4 Time budget, call cap and timeouts

- Kept: 8 minutes, 28 calls, 80 turns and a 240-second command timeout. The eval config matches the family's by hash (prefix 916cf6cb) [V]. The same 240-second value sets the final verification timeout, so lowering it is unsafe [V, harness source].
- Evidence: in V20 the 8-minute budget was spent in generation: 481.9 of 480 seconds, with 95.8 percent in completed requests, and only 7 of 28 calls used [V]. At eager speed time was the binding limit, not calls. The official speed is still unmeasured [V].
- Not adopted: a call cap of 32 (L3). It adds about +1 task, which is inside the noise, and its cost and gate were understated (section 3) [V, verifier].
- Held: D v5a, a call cap of 30 with the 9.8-second mean-per-call gate. Its central gain is +0.5, range 0 to +1.5 [I]. The earliest slot is 10-12, after the canary reads speed [V, ledger 10-10].
- Rule from the canary design: keep 28 calls unless the measured speed shows fewer than 10 percent of tasks hitting the 480-second cap [V, campaign plan].
- Repair named by the verifier: a call-cap gate must use total per-call time including tools, and a measured cap-hit fraction, on at least 30 non-eager tasks [V].

### 2.6 Compaction recovery (L1)

- Strategist proposal: replace the note requirement with a rule to recover state from git status after a summary. Expected gain 0 to +2, central +0.5 [I, strategist estimate].
- Verifiers (two independent sets) refuted it as written [V]:
  - The summariser input includes reasoning text, so the measure the strategy relied on, visible text beside tool calls, was the wrong channel.
  - The recovery rule omits the status tool. After a summary the agent does not know its remaining time or calls.
  - The central +0.5 is below one task. With 6 to 8 draws per arm the power to detect +0.5 is 0.07 to 0.10 [I].
  - The build would fail an existing contract test that checks the phrase it deletes.
- Repaired, not built: after any summary, first call the status tool, then git status and a bounded diff. Accept it only on process metrics: first-call compliance of at least 80 percent, no budget-blind turn, and no edit lost in an audit. Claim no score gain [I, verifier].
- Status: a canary-only prompt candidate (R6) is not in any official slot [V, strategy item 6].

### 2.7 Offload calibration (L4 and V21)

- Strategist proposal: a non-eager calibration canary to measure the official decode speed, as the prerequisite for the call-cap gate.
- Verifier: refuted as a standalone lever. Its measurement was already built as the V21 canary. Its cost was understated: three tasks at the 480-second cap take about 4,300 to 4,600 quota-seconds, not 3,000. Its rule used the median, while the envelope uses the mean [V].
- Speed ratio: the strategist's 1.73 times faster rested on a six-turn mean from one task. Our own eager runs give 1.3 to 2.4 times slower, depending on which turns count [V]. The official 8.68 seconds per call is a mean: 5,002 seconds of model time over about 576 implied calls, from a participant's post [P, arithmetic].
- Offload: to fit the notebook push limit, the bootstrap was moved to a private dataset. The largest accepted wire body was 1,036,069 bytes, and the smallest rejected was 1,181,475 bytes [V]. The dataset source was unversioned. On 10-09 the wheelhouse it resolved to had newer wheels, and the canary held at 135 seconds on 10-10 [V]. Offload is therefore an active risk.
- Status: the V21 calibration canary held. V22 was built but not pushed, and its push waited on the wheel-binding decision (plan, 10-10 11:30 UTC; the ledger records a re-bind decision at 11:27 UTC) [V].

## 3. Envelope arithmetic (the binding constraint)

Model: 120 tasks, 12.6 minutes setup, 0.5 minutes per task overhead, a 12-hour limit. Fractions are of the 12 hours, with cap-hit share f.

- At 8.7 seconds per call and 28 calls: 0.54, 0.64 and 0.78 for f of 0.3, 0.6 and 1.0. At 13 seconds per call: 0.76, 0.91 and 1.11 [I, strategist table].
- With every task at the cap, 32 calls fit 12 hours only if the per-call total is at most 10.1 seconds. At 10.9 seconds, a participant's figure including tool time, 32 calls give 12.84 hours [V, arithmetic].
- The break-even overrun probability for a +1 gain is 0.17 with a base of 5 tasks, or 0.09 with a base of 10 [V, arithmetic]. The verifier judged that the planned screen cannot bound the overrun probability below about 0.1 [I].

## 4. Predeclared readings and gates

- Thinking (A_think): 0.12 or more confirms; 0.08 to 0.10 inconclusive; 0.06 or less refutes [V, plan].
- Overflow switch (sweep lever 4): switch if there is any context error, or the 95th percentile of prompt plus 8,192 exceeds 30,000. V20 had no errors and a maximum of 23,185, so the rule was not met [V].
- Draw rule (sweep lever 2): 9 or more of 58 adopts a new base; 5 or fewer rejects; 6 to 8 repeats once [V, sweep].
- Call-cap gate: the strategist's rule was a projected 120-task total of at most 10 hours. The verifier found three different thresholds across the documents (10 s, 9 s and 10 hours). The coordinator set the D v5a gate at 9.8 seconds mean per call, the stricter reading [V].
- Canary gate items (plan): items 1 and 4 failed in V19 and V20. The coordinator waived item 4 on 10-08 at 17:25 UTC as hardware-bound, and waived the status and arm-exit parts of item 1 on 17:35 UTC as a consequence [V].
